# Banoffee Pie (summary)

Serves: 8–10
Difficulty: Easy
Prep: 30 mins
Cook: 30–35 mins

## Ingredients

- 4 bananas, sliced
- 394 g caramel or dulce de leche (about one can)
- 300 ml double cream
- Dark chocolate, to grate (optional)

For the pastry:

- 100 g cold butter (plus extra for greasing)
- 200 g plain flour
- 1 medium egg, separated
- 1 tbsp golden caster sugar

## Method (summary)

1. Make the pastry: put the chilled butter and plain flour in a food processor and pulse until the mixture resembles fresh breadcrumbs. Add the egg yolk and the sugar and pulse again to combine. Add very cold water, 1 tbsp at a time, pulsing after each addition until the dough just comes together into one piece.

2. Remove the dough, bring together gently with your hands and form a smooth ball. Wrap tightly in cling film and chill in the fridge for 30 minutes.

3. Heat the oven to 190°C (170°C fan)/gas 4. Grease a 23 cm loose-bottomed fluted tart tin.

4. Roll out the chilled pastry on a lightly floured surface and use to line the tin. Leave about ½ cm of pastry proud of the edge and trim with scissors.

5. Line the base with baking parchment and weigh down with baking beans or uncooked rice. Blind-bake for 15 minutes. Remove the paper and beans, brush the pastry base with the beaten egg white (from the reserved egg) and return to the oven for a further 15–20 minutes until crisp, golden and cooked through. Leave to cool completely (can be made a day ahead).

6. Assemble: spread half the caramel over the cooled pastry case and layer the sliced bananas on top. Cover with the remaining caramel and chill in the fridge.

7. Whip the double cream until billowy-thick and spoon over the top of the tart just before serving. Finely grate a little dark chocolate over the cream to garnish, if using.

## Tips

- The tart case can be baked a day ahead and kept wrapped until ready to fill.
- BBC Good Food recommends a generous dollop of cream when serving.
- Use ready-made dulce de leche or a can of caramel for convenience.
